## 💎 The Bento Grid Philosophy: Visual Hierarchy Redefined

Why a Bento-style UI? It's more than just an aesthetic choice—it's **Information UX Psychology**:

1. **Cognitive Load Reduction**: By modularizing information into distinct "tiles," we prevent "Wall of Text" fatigue.
2. **Visual Hierarchy**: Our "Neural Logic" determines which tile expands into a **Hero Card**. High-value intelligence is visually prioritized, ensuring your eyes land on what matters first.
3. **Responsive Density**: The Bento layout adapts its density dynamically. Whether you're on a 4K monitor or a smartphone, the information hierarchy remains crystal clear.
4. **Glassmorphism Aesthetic**: Modern CSS backdrop filters create a "Cyber-Glass" feel that makes data feel alive and premium.
